Erlenmeyer Flask

Also known as: Conical Flask

Specifications

Size Options 25ml, 50ml, 100ml, 250ml, 500ml, 1000ml
Material Borosilicate Glass
Type Narrow Neck, Wide Neck
Temperature Range -70°C to 500°C
Location Cabinet A3, Shelf 2

Purpose/Common Uses

  • Mixing and stirring solutions
  • Heating liquids
  • Containing chemical reactions
  • Titrations
  • Preparing solutions

Proper Handling and Storage

  • Inspect for cracks before each use
  • Heat gradually to prevent thermal shock
  • Do not heat thick-walled flasks over direct flame
  • Store in designated cabinets away from edges
  • Clean immediately after use with appropriate solvents

Maintenance Procedures

  • Wash with laboratory detergent and rinse thoroughly
  • For persistent residues, soak in appropriate cleaning solution
  • Rinse multiple times with deionized water
  • Dry in inverted position on drying rack
  • Periodic annealing may be required for heavily used flasks

Sulfuric Acid

Also known as: Oil of Vitriol, Battery Acid

Chemical Information

Chemical Formula H₂SO₄
CAS Number 7664-93-9
Molecular Weight 98.079 g/mol
Concentration 98% (Concentrated), 0.5M, 1.0M, 2.0M (Solutions)
Location Acid Cabinet A, Shelf 1 (Ventilated)

Hazard Information

  • Corrosive to skin and eyes
  • Causes severe burns
  • Harmful if inhaled
  • Reacts violently with water (always add acid to water)
  • Environmental hazard

Storage Requirements

  • Store in corrosion-resistant cabinet with secondary containment
  • Keep container tightly closed
  • Store away from bases, metals, and organic materials
  • Ventilated area required
  • Maximum storage temperature: 30°C

Compatibility Information

Do not store with:

  • Bases (e.g., sodium hydroxide, potassium hydroxide)
  • Metals (e.g., zinc, aluminum)
  • Organic materials
  • Water
  • Chlorates, perchlorates, permanganates

Common Laboratory Applications

  • Acid catalyzed reactions
  • Dehydrating agent
  • pH adjustment
  • Cleaning agent for glassware
  • Battery electrolyte
