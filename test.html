Electrochemistry Formulas

Cell Potential: E°cell = E°cathode - E°anode

Nernst Equation: E = E° - (RT/nF)ln(Q)

Faraday's Law: m = (M × I × t)/(n × F)

where:

  • m = mass of substance (g)
  • M = molar mass (g/mol)
  • I = current (A)
  • t = time (s)
  • n = electrons transferred
  • F = Faraday constant (96,485 C/mol)
